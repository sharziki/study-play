# Intellect — Visual Narrative Contract

## Transformation
From an overwhelming pile of material to one precise next challenge. The interface should feel like entering a quiet training chamber: no planning overhead, no visual noise, no ambiguity about what to do now.

## Recurring visual metaphor
**The mastery field** — a constellation/grid of topic nodes that brighten as knowledge becomes retrievable. It appears as the dashboard's topic field, condenses into the current topic signal during a sprint, and resolves into a mastery delta after each answer.

## Product chapters
1. **Command** — Today's attack queue, readiness, and one primary Start Sprint action.
2. **Focus** — one full-width challenge with progress, pace, mode, and keyboard controls.
3. **Correction** — answer, explanation, source evidence, confidence calibration, and mastery change.
4. **Map** — tracks, materials, topic mastery, and due pressure.
5. **Intake** — paste/upload source material with track assignment and immediate generation.

## Art direction
- Dark-native, precision-engineered interface inspired by Linear's information hierarchy, not its identity.
- Near-black canvas; elevated graphite surfaces; warm off-white text.
- One electric periwinkle accent for active states and a restrained amber for transfer/boss rounds.
- Inter for interface and editorial serif accents only in problem statements where they improve mathematical reading.
- Thin luminous borders, almost no drop shadows, 8px rhythm, 8/12/18px radii.
- Nyx appears as a tiny ambient signal, not a cartoon occupying study space.

## Motion
- 160–220ms transitions for queue-to-focus and reveal states.
- Mastery delta sweeps across the topic signal after grading.
- No scroll hijacking, no animation during typing, and full reduced-motion support.

## Desktop
Persistent 248px rail; dashboard uses a wide command area plus compact right evidence rail. Focus mode collapses navigation and centers a 760–900px challenge column.

## Mobile
Single-column command view; persistent bottom Start action; focus mode uses the entire viewport; confidence and grade controls remain thumb-sized. No desktop-only hover dependency.

## Accessibility
Visible focus rings, semantic buttons/forms, 44px minimum interactive targets, high contrast, keyboard shortcuts with button equivalents, aria-live feedback, reduced-motion fallback, and no color-only status encoding.
